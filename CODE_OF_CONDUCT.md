# Code of Conduct

Cluub follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, respectful and constructive; assume good intent; no harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or publishing others' private information. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space representing the project.

**Reporting**: contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (the `cluub-lang` organization owners). Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove content or block participants who violate this code.
